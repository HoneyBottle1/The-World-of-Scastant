## The War of the Lost Son
The War of the Lost Son was a conflict opposing the Tiondywarm Kingdom and Azraine and lasting 37 years, from 660 ER to 697 ER.
In 29 MLS 660 ER, The sixteen-year-old Oliver Hillbrand, son to the infamous King Basil Hillbrand, was captured by a group of bandits who claimed were Azraine insurrectionists. Oliver was captured in the dead of night of that fateful night, his captors having snuck past the outer piers of the port of Stonerest. They infiltrated the royal palace, and quickly seized the boy without a noise, leaving only a note for the King to find. The next morning, the nurse found the bed empty, save for that note, which she brought as quickly as she could to the King in the dining hall. 
When he read the note, King Basil stood up in rage and immediately commanded a meeting to be held with his generals.
